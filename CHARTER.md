# V4 research charter — frozen before experiments

Created 2026-10-21 UTC. Astra manages three Sol workstreams: data/provenance, model/causal validation, execution/paper workflow. Original v2 and other projects remain unchanged; original SHA256 inventory is preserved. All new work stays in this folder. This is research/paper only: no broker or real orders.

Objective: assess whether a cash-only $5,000 strategy can add economic value over SPY total-return buy-and-hold and the same-horizon simple strategy, and approach five times positive annual SPY return after costs. The 5x aim is aspirational, not promised. Preserve 5% trade/session with 15% weekly and 10% trade/session with 30% weekly loss limits; target maximum drawdown 10%; max three positions, two candidates/session, one new entry/session. No leverage or raised risk. A single predeclared LOWER-risk variant (half position sizing) may be included only for the frozen finalist as sensitivity, never selected to conceal failures.

Hypotheses: (H1) executable 20-session bracket outcomes align the training target with actual stops and take-profits better than fixed returns; (H2) bounded newly acquired volatility/credit/rate context can improve conditional entry quality beyond existing price/volume information; (H3) calibrated uncertainty/no-entry decisions improve realized economics; (H4) apparent improvement may merely be market exposure, which requires explicit entry-filter and exposure ablations.

Budget: at most SIX new candidate definitions, including price-only control, target alignment, context additions and confidence/entry-filter ablations. Fix alpha=100 and horizon=20 (no new hyperparameter grid). Models must be simple Ridge or logistic pipelines with train-only scaling, expanding training from 2010, quarterly refits, matured labels and ten full session embargo. The exact candidate registry and data availability decisions must be written before any candidate performance is inspected. Maximum 200 portfolio evaluations and 1,000 fits; stop once those declared candidates and sensitivities are evaluated. No rescue search or final-history retuning. Agent must count actual fits/runs.

Chronology: 2010–2018 initial fit, 2019–2021 development, 2022–2023 validation, 2024–2026-10-19 final descriptive evaluation. ALL 2019 onward dates have already been repeatedly inspected; none are untouched. New feature history does not reset that fact. Candidate ranking uses development only: first number of risk/cost scenarios beating BOTH comparable SPY and samehold baseline while max DD <=10%; then worst scenario CAGR minus max(benchmark CAGR) minus twice excess DD over10%; then lower turnover and lexical ID. Lock one research finalist before validation/final. Validation may reject but cannot replace finalist. If none pass, keep best-ranked only as a rejected research artifact, never approved model.

Acceptance gates: base and stressed after-cost profitability; max DD <=10% in base and stress; positive increment over SPY passive and samehold simple in each risk-policy base scenario; exploratory block-bootstrap paired increment lower95% bound >0 versus both; >=100 completed final trades; positive SPY full calendar years require strategy annual return >=5x SPY annual return; nonpositive SPY years report excess percentage points and downside without ratio. Partial 2026 YTD is marked partial and excluded from full-calendar-year five-times gate. Report every gate, including failures. Do not silently substitute >10% CAGR for goal. Portfolio/model fitting metrics alone cannot pass.

Required output: reproducible code, new acquired raw data with URLs retrieval timestamps SHA256 units release lags and unavailable sources, original-data audit, fixed registry and events, all failed candidates/folds and validation distributions, credible total-return benchmarks, yearly return/multiple, CAGR net P/L DD/duration loss streaks turnover/cost/stress sample/exposure, dependence-aware uncertainty explicitly exploratory, saved pipeline if useful, local paper/replay command refusing stale/missing inputs and unsupported live use, meaningful tests, source/output manifests, final comparative report. Corrections require an explicit defect record and rerunning affected artifacts without weakening gates.
