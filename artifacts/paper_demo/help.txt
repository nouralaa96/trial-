usage: paper.py [-h] [--bundle BUNDLE] [--date DATE] [--as-of AS_OF]
                [--scenario SCENARIO] [--snapshot SNAPSHOT]
                [--snapshot-out SNAPSHOT_OUT] [--output OUTPUT]
                {replay,paper,live,order,status}

Local research replay and guarded paper inspection. No order or broker API. A
bundle binds forecasts, model, gates, config and input files by SHA256.
Historical replay reconstructs the selected portfolio from its frozen period
start; it does not fabricate an empty account or use a final model to back-
predict past dates.

positional arguments:
  {replay,paper,live,order,status}

options:
  -h, --help            show this help message and exit
  --bundle BUNDLE
  --date DATE
  --as-of AS_OF
  --scenario SCENARIO
  --snapshot SNAPSHOT
  --snapshot-out SNAPSHOT_OUT
  --output OUTPUT
