# V5 first diagnostic: where V4's return gap arose

This is a read-only analysis of the frozen V4 final `risk05_base_whole` ledgers, $5,000 starting capital, 2024-01-02 to 2026-10-19. Run `.venv/bin/python analysis/v5/attribute_v4.py --output analysis/v5/attribution.json` to regenerate the exact accounting report. The script checks each strategy's closed-trade P/L, final equity, fees and friction against `final_metrics.json`, checks identical valuation dates, and reconciles every displayed C03/T05 difference. It does not refit or select a model.

\*The gross column adds recorded fees and fill friction back to net P/L. It is an accounting bridge, **not** a hypothetical rerun with zero transaction costs.

T05 exceeds C03 by **$2,174.38 net**. Its before-cost advantage is **$2,203.08**, reduced by **$28.69** of additional modeled cost. C03 trails SPY by **$1,640.64 net**. Thus fees and modeled slippage do not explain why Ridge trails the simple rule. The two active strategies have broadly similar average marked exposure, so a large difference in overall invested time is not the obvious explanation either. This does not control for which asset was held on which day.

An accounting partition by identical symbol and entry date finds only **7 shared entries**; all 7 have the same exit date. The shared entries together favor C03 by $191.69. T05's 51 distinct entries earned $3,367.30, including **26 GLD trades earning $2,851.49**. C03's 40 distinct entries earned $1,001.22, including 11 QQQ trades losing $453.09. These amounts reconcile exactly to the $2,174.38 net gap. In total, C03 earned $1,272.60 from 11 GLD trades; T05 earned $3,088.57 from 30 GLD trades. The largest T05-only GLD trade earned $906.92, so a few opportunities have a material influence on the comparison.

This identifies **different entry selection and subsequent portfolio paths** as the principal descriptive gap. It does not prove that copying T05's isolated entries into C03 would have yielded those profits: positions compete for cash and risk room, quantities change with prior equity, and exits can change after substitutions. Common entries with identical exit dates offer no sign here that a different exit rule explains the observed aggregate gap. A causal entry or exit change requires a separately predeclared counterfactual engine run and then future evidence; it must not be optimized on this already viewed final period.

C03 had 22 winning and 25 losing completed trades, a longest loss streak of 4, and a worst underwater run of 182 sessions. T05 had 27 winning and 31 losing trades, a longest loss streak of 5, and a 96-session underwater run. The 10% drawdown gate failed for both active strategies in this final scenario (C03 13.40%, T05 13.26%). C03's closed-trade exits include 19 regular stop losses totaling −$2,197.06 and three opening-gap stops totaling −$808.78; 21 time exits total +$3,159.40. Stop exits being negative is expected and does not by itself establish an exit defect. The generated JSON contains per-symbol, exit-reason and exit-year breakdowns for each strategy; exit-year trade P/L is **not** that year's marked account return.
